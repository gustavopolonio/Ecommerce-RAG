# DOC-004 — Product Warranty
Store: DemoShop | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-11-01

- Physical electronic products sold by DemoShop have a 12-month limited warranty starting from the delivery date.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use.
- Accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse, and normal wear are excluded.
- Books, stationery, and non-electronic accessories do not include this limited electronics warranty; statutory rights may still apply.
- Customers seeking warranty assistance should contact support with an order number, product name, and description of the problem.
- The assistant cannot approve a replacement or confirm coverage for an individual item without review by support.
