# DOC-001 — Payment Policy
Store: DemoShop | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-11-01

- Accepted payment methods: Pix, credit cards (Visa and Mastercard), and debit cards (Visa and Mastercard).
- Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies are not accepted.
- Credit card purchases can be paid in up to 3 interest-free installments, for orders of $60 or more. Purchases below $60 must be paid in full.
- A single order cannot be split across multiple payment methods. Combining debit and credit cards is not supported.
- Pix, debit cards, and credit cards paid in full require a single payment.
- Payment options are shown at checkout. The assistant cannot verify individual payments or transactions.
