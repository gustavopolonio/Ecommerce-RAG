# DOC-006 — Frequently Asked Questions
Store: DemoShop | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-11-01

**Do you accept Pix?** Yes. See DOC-001 for all payment conditions.

**Can I combine a debit card and credit card?** No. One payment method per order (DOC-001).

**Can I return something after 40 days?** The standard return window is 30 calendar days after delivery. Ask support about possible warranty issues, not an unauthorized refund (DOC-002 and DOC-004).

**Do you ship worldwide?** No, only within the United States (DOC-003).

**Where is my order?** Use your order confirmation/tracking link or contact support. This assistant cannot access order records (DOC-003).

**Can I buy books?** Yes; see DOC-005 for the listed titles.

**Can I cancel an order?** Contact human support. Cancellation eligibility cannot be determined from this knowledge base; do not promise a cancellation or refund.
