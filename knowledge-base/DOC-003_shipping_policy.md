# DOC-003 — Shipping Policy
Store: DemoShop | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-11-01

- DemoShop ships only within the United States; international and worldwide shipping are not available.
- Standard shipping: estimated 5–8 business days after dispatch, $5.00 per order.
- Express shipping: estimated 2–3 business days after dispatch, $12.00 per order.
- Free standard shipping applies to orders of $75 or more.
- Orders usually dispatch within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation.
- Estimates are not guarantees. The exact delivery date cannot be calculated from a ZIP code alone with the current knowledge base.
- The assistant has no live shipping calculator, customer location access, or order-tracking integration. For order-specific delivery questions, direct the customer to the order confirmation/tracking link or human support.
