# DOC-002 — Return & Refund Policy
Store: DemoShop | Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-11-01

- Customers may request a standard return within 30 calendar days after delivery.
- Returns requested after 30 days are not eligible for a standard refund. The assistant cannot authorize exceptions.
- To qualify, items must be unused, undamaged, and returned with their original accessories and packaging.
- Digital gift cards and personalized products are non-returnable unless required by applicable law.
- To request a return, contact support with the order number and reason for return. Support reviews eligibility and supplies return instructions.
- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item is inspected. Bank processing may take additional time.
- If an item is defective, customers may contact support about warranty coverage even after the standard return window.
